# Visual Release Criteria

Apply within [Brix's shared scoped pass](../../../AGENT.md#scoped-inspection-policy), not as another review. Inspect only changed elements and direct effects; reuse current available evidence. These criteria supplement actual structural data.

## Composition and shared identity

Compare changed composition with the pinned source/baseline and intended adaptations at useful scale. Check masses, proportions, alignment, whitespace, density and action placement. Matching control inventory alone is insufficient. Use the same product typography, colors and component families across references; flag observed inconsistency rather than demanding an unrelated full-product audit.

## Legibility and state

At intended display size inspect affected edges, masks, text, Arabic dots/diacritics, safe areas, shadows and overlaps. Distinguish intentional crop/scroll from meaningful clipping. Preserve appropriate masks rather than disabling them globally. Check font size/weight, background contrast, RTL/mixed order and required state/sizing behavior. Calculate any claimed contrast ratio from actual values; do not guess metrics.

Form snapshot fields, constraints, messages, strength indicators, consent, countdown/resend and button state must agree with the canonical policy. Do not invent backend rules or retrospectively change the policy to excuse inconsistency. Static frames do not prove working validation or interactions.

## Product imagery and previews

Required product slots use real photographs with known provenance. No generated lookalikes, icons or geometric product substitutes. A known inventory item needs verified supplied/official/authorized imagery; conceptual stock photography is labeled illustrative. Decorative artwork is separate. Check actual placement as part of changed output, reusing source inspection.

A foundation preview uses representative realistic content and required assets to judge the proposal, not isolated swatches alone or an unsolicited entire library. Keep token/documentation labels outside simulated product copy. Temporary assets cannot pass required final imagery criteria.

## Mockups

Only when a mockup is requested/needed, use a visibly rounded shell and aligned content clipping with a fixed 24px radius, independent of width/height, unless the user supplies another fixed value. Preserve editable screen content and safe areas. Avoid sharp leaked backgrounds and scaled baked-in rounding. Use structural values plus the actual shared visual result; do not apply this radius to unrelated product controls or clip the outer board.

## Findings

Meaningful clipping, illegible required text, unsuitable product substitutes, incoherent states and failed required composition are defects, not optional polish. Store each finding once with affected scope, evidence and required correction. Resolve from the repair's affected after-pass evidence; keep independent valid work usable. No extra acceptance inspection or intermediate-layout history is required.
