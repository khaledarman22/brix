# Research and Selection

Follow [Brix](../../AGENT.md) for when research is needed, per-screen quality, identity and inspection policy. This one task owns strategy, discovery, assessment, comparison and selection. Do not invoke the legacy aliases serially. Simple edits skip research unless explicitly requested; research-only tasks do not authorize design.

## Search loop

1. Restore scope/screens, platform, requirements, identity, supplied references, rejections, previous routes and cumulative budget. Keep short search intent in the canonical selection record.
2. Search small batches by composition, hierarchy, proportions, whitespace and content capacity. Include domain-neutral and cross-industry routes; function/industry matching is not a quality bonus. Distinguish the actual depicted platform, not the host gallery.
3. Inspect promising original visuals once at readable detail with relevant context. Reuse actual available evidence. In that pass compare visual strength, platform/content fit, typography, balance, legibility and adaptability to shared components. A thumbnail, source popularity or description does not prove unseen properties. Static visuals do not prove behavior.
4. Choose the strongest sufficient coherent set. Map every new screen's overall composition and important regions to inspected selections with a short fit rationale. One source can cover several screens when suitable; no quota of unique sources. Reject weak/unfit choices even if they match the subject. Keep only identifying link/ID and short rejection reason to avoid repeated routes.
5. Continue targeted search for uncovered screens within the same task, varying weak routes. Keep them blocked; independently covered screens may proceed. Stop when coverage is sufficient. Default effort ceilings are 12 queries, 30 unique candidates and 8 deeply reviewed candidates, not quotas. Retain counters across resumes. If a self-selected ceiling is inadequate, record a justified finite extension before continuing; increasing a user-imposed cap requires their authorization. Never reset counters or lower quality to finish. At real access/effort limits report the remaining gap and next action.

New designs/features, material redesigns and explicitly requested alternative references need scoped discovery. Prior sources may support comparison; do not label them newly found. Honor explicitly supplied-reference-only scope. UI inspiration does not supply factual product requirements or license screenshots for production. Product-stock photography is a separate asset task.

## One record

Use existing canonical selection storage; new tasks normally use `research/REFERENCE_MEMORY.md#<task-id>`. Keep:

- Scope/screens, search intent, executed route/cursor and budget counters.
- Selection IDs, original source/attribution, image version and useful region or whole-image scope.
- Short fit/quality reason, intended use and material adapt/avoid constraints.
- Links to actual inspection/provenance plus ready/blocked screen/part coverage.
- Rejected IDs/reasons, unresolved gaps and next action.

Asset metadata and inspection outcomes stay with their owner; reference them rather than copying. No independent brief, rankings, review report or shortlist handoff is required. Existing legacy records remain readable; do not migrate them just to match this format.

Use exact source-pixel geometry only when a region needs it. Reuse originals and existing derivatives. Marked images/crops are optional communication aids, not automatic outputs; full-image selection needs no identical crop. Check only newly created derivative scope without reopening an unchanged original. [Shared assets](../shared-visual-assets/SKILL.md) defines registration and [the contract](../shared-visual-assets/references/package-contract.md) defines fields when needed.

## Apply or respond

Pass the canonical record reference and ready scope to application. It adds only new content/identity/composition decisions, using shared inspection evidence. Present alternatives only on request or for a consequential unresolved choice; autonomous selection is not user approval. Rejections and explicit new-reference requests update affected scope only. No completion while required screen coverage is silently missing.
